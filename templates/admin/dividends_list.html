How dividends work
  1. The dashboard now shows both collected pool and projected pool for the selected year.
  2. Projected pool uses loan interest for loans in the year and year penalties.
  3. The top saver of the year is awarded an extra {{ CONFIG.TOP_SAVER_AWARD|money }}.
  4. The remaining amount is divided equally among all active members.
  5. Admin and Treasurer can run or rerun yearly distribution to refresh projections.
