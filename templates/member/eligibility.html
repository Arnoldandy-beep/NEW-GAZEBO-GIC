Guarantor selection policy

Choose members who have already agreed to guarantee your loan. Guarantor savings balances are intentionally not displayed in the member portal.

How loan security works
  1. Your own available savings are locked first as collateral.
  2. If the loan exceeds your savings, the difference is split between your two guarantors' available savings.
  3. Loans are due on the 30th of the agreed month and accrue {{ rate_pct }}% interest per month plus penalty if overdue.
How to Apply for a Loan
  1. Obtain a loan application form from the Treasurer or Secretary.
  2. Complete the form and nominate two (2) GIC members as guarantors.
  3. Submit the signed form to the Treasurer.
  4. Upon verification, the Treasurer will capture the application in the system.
  5. The Loan Committee — Chairman, Treasurer, Secretary, and Committee Member — reviews all requests. Any three (3) approvals will activate the loan.
  6. Approval progress is visible in real time once the application is submitted.
