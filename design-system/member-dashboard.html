Design intent

Warm, personal, one-thumb

Every card answers one member question in the order they ask it: Am I paid up? How much have I saved? What do I owe? What do I get? The navy hero carries identity and balance; everything below is white space and rounded cards so the screen never feels like an admin console.

Encouraging, never punitive

Arrears use amber-first language (“Unpaid”, not “Defaulter”), paired with a clear amount and a single action. Green is reserved for genuine progress — streaks, cleared fees, on-track status — so it keeps meaning.

Scales to desktop untouched

The same cards reflow into a two-column grid above 900px: hero full-width, savings + loan side by side, fines and dividend in the right rail. No separate mobile template needed in Flask — one member/dashboard.html with .gic-grid breakpoints.

Screen anatomy

1 · Greeting hero
Avatar, date, member number, savings balance, growth chip.
2 · This month's savings
Paid/pending chip, receipt line, 12-month streak strip.
3 · Quick actions
Deposit · Statement · Apply for loan · Minutes.
4 · Savings trend
Gold area line, 12-month window, plain-language delta.
5 · Active loan
Outstanding, % repaid donut, next payment alert, CTA.
6 · Fines & arrears
Itemised with status chips and an outstanding total.
7 · Dividend outlook
Gold-tinted card — the reward for staying compliant.
8 · Club sitting notice
Community touch: next meeting, venue, agenda link.

Copy & number rules

UGXAlways prefix, grouped in thousands, never abbreviate on member screens (admin KPIs may use M).
Dates“5 Sep 2026” plus a relative hint (“in 9 days”) for anything actionable.
ToneSecond person, active voice: “You've saved 8 of 12 months”, not “Member compliance: 66%”.
