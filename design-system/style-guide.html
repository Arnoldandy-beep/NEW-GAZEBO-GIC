Design language
Gazebo Premium

A member-owned savings club is not a bank. The system should read as trustworthy and precise like a financial institution, but warm and collective like the people who fund it — deep navy for authority, gold for shared reward, generous rounding and soft light for approachability.

Gold is a signature, not a workhorse
Gold carries brand identity, money-moving actions and reward states. Cap it at roughly 10% of any screen; never use it for body text on white (use gold-600 and only above 12px/700).

Density

Admin screens: 12px row padding, 18px card padding, 16px gutters. Member screens: +25% — 16px rows, 18–20px card padding, 44px minimum tap targets.

Elevation & surface

shadow-1 · resting cards
shadow-2 · hover, dropdowns
shadow-3 · hero, modals
shadow-gold · gold CTA only

Two-layer shadows tinted navy (rgba(13,27,48,…)), never neutral black. In dark mode shadows deepen instead of lightening; separation comes from the surface → surface-2 → surface-3 ladder plus a 12% white hairline.

Weights

ph (regular) for navigation and buttons; ph-light for large decorative or empty-state glyphs; ph-fill only inside status chips and alerts where the icon must read at 12px.

Sizes

12 in chips · 16 in buttons · 17 in sidebar · 18 in card headers · 21–24 in tile badges and quick actions. Always line-height:1 and vertical-align:-.125em.

Colour

Icons inherit text colour except card-header icons (gold-500) and tile badges, which take the tile's semantic tint on a 10–14% tinted square (11px radius).

Principles & guardrails

Money first, chrome second
The largest element on any screen is a number a member cares about.
Colour carries meaning, not decoration
Green/amber/red are reserved for state. A card is never coloured just to look lively.
Cooperative, not corporate
Real member names, receipts, meeting notices and streaks — the club is visible in the UI.
Dense for leadership, roomy for members
Same components, two densities; never ship an admin table to a member's phone.
Dark mode is first-class
Every component is authored against tokens, so no screen needs a dark-specific override.
DO
  • One hero, one gold CTA per screen
  • Tabular figures for every amount
  • Status as a chip, never as raw text
  • Empty states with a next action
DON'T
  • Gradient-fill KPI cards in rainbow colours
  • Mix icon sets (no Bootstrap Icons + Phosphor)
  • Stack more than two shadows levels
  • Use red for anything that isn't a breach
