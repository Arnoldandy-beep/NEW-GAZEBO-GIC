Design system
Deliverable 3

Component & theme kit

Every pattern below renders in light and dark side by side. Apply the same classes across Loans, Savings, Fines, Notifications, Minutes, Members, Financial Statements and Settings — no module-specific CSS.

01

Hero banner template

One per page, directly under the topbar. Slots: eyebrow, headline figure or title, status chips, supporting line, and up to four inline stats. Orbs and rings are decorative <span>s — never images.

02

KPI / stat tiles

Default (gold rail) plus .is-positive .is-warning .is-danger .is-info .is-purple and .feature. Optional sparkline, meter or split meter in the footer slot.

03

Status chips & role badges

Pill shape, 11.5px, 700 weight, soft tinted background + 1px matching border. Meaning is fixed system-wide: green = settled, blue = in progress, amber = needs attention, red = breach, gold = club/reward, purple = system.

04

Buttons & quick actions

Navy gradient = primary commit action. Gold = money-moving or celebratory action (deposit, repay, declare dividend) — at most one per view. Ghost/subtle for everything else.

05

Data table

Sticky uppercase header, 12px row padding, hairline dividers, tabular-figure money columns right-aligned, and a 3px left rail (.row-flag-danger) for rows that need action. Used identically in Members, Savings, Loans, Fines and Statements.

06

Chart style

Three chart types only. Navy = money in / principal, gold = money out / interest, green = net or positive outcome, red = at risk. Rounded bar caps (5px), 2.5px line weight, hairline grid, no chart borders, no 3D, no legends inside the plot.

07

Forms & inputs

10px radius, 1px border, gold focus ring (the accent members already associate with action). Currency fields always use the UGX prefix add-on so amounts are never ambiguous.

08

Alerts, empty states & feeds

Empty-state illustrations are inline SVG built from the same geometry as the brand mark — a gazebo roof, coin stack and ledger — stroked with --gic-ink-400 and --gic-gold-500 so they re-colour automatically in dark mode.

09

Navigation shell

Sidebar stays navy in both themes — it is the brand anchor. Active item gets a gold left rail, gold icon and a 12% gold wash. Topbar is translucent with a blur so content scrolls under it.
